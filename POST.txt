A word on reading that table: the top rows are not the best rows.
These are binary contracts, so what you pay scales with how likely
the outcome already looks. A variant winning 82% of the time is
buying at around 85c and losing money; one winning 56% and buying
at 53c is making it. Win rate on its own tells you nothing here --
it is the gap between the win rate and the price that pays, and
the price is not in this file.
